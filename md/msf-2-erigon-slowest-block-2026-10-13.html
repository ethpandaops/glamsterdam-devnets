    Method and caveats

    • Erigon timings: 48,781 [5/5 Execution] parallel done log lines, taken from ClickHouse external.otel_logs for the six Erigon nodes. Every execution is logged, re-executions included. "First execution" is each node's first log line for a block.
    • Other clients: xatu execution_engine_new_payload samples only about a third of blocks per node, and recent rows can still be arriving. That is why some clients have only one or two samples per block.
    • Block metadata: gas, transaction count and slot come from the Dora /api/v1/slots endpoint.
    • Hardware: results can depend on the host. They do not establish a fair cross-client throughput ranking.
